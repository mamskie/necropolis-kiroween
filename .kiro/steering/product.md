# Steering — Product

- Necropolis resurrects the personal homepage, not the social profile.
  Every feature must answer: "would this have existed on GeoCities in
  1998?" If not, it does not belong.
- The app is the editor AND the artifact: what you build is a real,
  publishable homepage, not a mockup.
- Fun first, polish second. A slightly ugly, charming page beats a
  sterile perfect one.
- No accounts, no servers, no tracking. The user's page is theirs.
