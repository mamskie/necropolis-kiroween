# Steering — Style

- 90s web voice in the generated copy: "Welcome to my corner of the
  cyberspace!", "Best viewed at 800x600", "Under construction",
  "Sign my guestbook or be cursed".
- Editor chrome is modern and calm (dark, readable) so the preview's
  chaos is the star.
- Themes are CSS-only: no images, no webfonts, no external assets.
- Buttons and labels are plain English; the spookiness lives in the
  preview, not in the tooling.
